Discover Soul Market

Soul Market is one of the few places in Ialoveni where we can escape the constant urban environment, while enjoying the city at the same time. Soul Market is a unique place, not only for its variety of products, but also for its wonderful existence, a variety of daily life.

Good Quality

Each set is a selection of the most exclusive local products. All are taken from the country's natural environment, bringing not only exceptional quality, but also supporting local farmers and producers.

Friendly Place

Soul Market is also a fabulous place to spend time with the family, the mini bookstore, playground and conventional chairs are perfect for drinking tea or coffee with your loved ones.

Custom Options

Each set is a mix of selections, and when we want a more special gift, we have the option to customize our orders, for ourselves and for our loved ones.
